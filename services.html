What Ageta Offers

Thoughtful Design, Any Budget

Every project — whether a full custom home or a single room renovation — gets the same hands-on attention. Good design shouldn't be reserved for the largest budgets; it should be tailored to yours.

01

Custom Home Design

A home designed entirely around how you live — from the first sketch of the floor plan to the final finishes. Ageta works with you from concept through construction, ensuring every space serves a real purpose in your daily life.

Best for: Clients building on their own land, ready to start from a blank slate.

02

Renovations & Additions

Reimagine or expand your existing space without starting from scratch. Whether it's a single room refresh or a full second-floor addition, Ageta designs renovations that respect your home's existing character while solving real space or flow problems.

Best for: Homeowners who love their location but have outgrown their layout.

03

Design Consultation

Not ready to commit to a full project? A design consultation gives you expert guidance — reviewing existing plans, advising on layout decisions, or simply helping you understand what's possible before you invest further.

Best for: Clients early in the process who want expert input before committing.

04

Site Planning & Feasibility

Own land but not sure what's buildable? Ageta assesses your site's potential — topography, access, budget realities — so you can make informed decisions before design even begins.

Best for: Landowners who need clarity before starting a project.

How It Works

The Full Process, Step by Step

01

Consult

We start with a conversation — your vision, your budget, your site, and how you actually live day to day. This is the foundation everything else is built on.

02

Design

Ageta creates a design tailored specifically to you — floor plans, elevations, and material choices reflecting how you'll use every space. You review and refine it together.

03

Build

Once approved, Ageta guides construction directly — coordinating with contractors, reviewing progress on-site, and solving problems as they arise.

04

Move In

The final walkthrough isn't a formality — it's where every detail is checked against what was promised, before handing you the keys.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Ageta only for high-budget projects?

Not at all. Every project — regardless of size or budget — gets the same hands-on attention and design care. Ageta works with you to find the best possible outcome within your specific budget.

I don't know where to start. Is that a problem?

Not at all — most clients start exactly there. The first consultation is designed to help you figure out what's possible, what you actually need, and how to move forward, even if you're starting with more questions than answers.

Will Ageta actually listen to what I want, or push his own style?

Your home should reflect how you live, not a signature style imposed on every project. Ageta's process starts with listening — your input shapes the design from the very first sketch.

How involved do I need to be during construction?

As involved as you'd like to be. Ageta manages day-to-day coordination and problem-solving on site, keeping you updated at key milestones — so you're informed without needing to manage contractors yourself.
