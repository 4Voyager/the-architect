His Story

Long before his first blueprint, there were notebooks full of floor plans.

Every architect has an origin story. Ageta's starts well before his first client — with a childhood fascination for how spaces come together, and a stubborn belief that good design shouldn't be reserved for the wealthy few. This is how a kid who filled notebooks with floor plans became the solo architect guiding clients across Kenya today.

The Journey

From First Sketch to Solo Practice

01

A Childhood Spent Drawing

Long before he understood architecture as a profession, Ageta filled notebooks with floor plans — dream houses sketched for the fun of it.

02

Choosing the Path

He pursued architecture with an uncommon ambition: not to build the biggest firm, but the best relationship with every client he'd ever work with.

03

The First Client, The Hard Years

His first real project came from a family friend willing to take a chance on him. Building a solo practice meant proving, project by project, that hands-on and high-quality weren't mutually exclusive.

04

Where He Stands Today

12 years later, that same philosophy carries into every home he designs — still hands-on, still listening first, at any budget.
